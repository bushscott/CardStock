Logo

Two fanned cards, a sparkline drawn across the front one, and a filled dot at the latest point. The mark is the product's thesis in 32 pixels: cardboard, measured.

The card proportion is 15:21 — near enough to a real trading card (2.5 × 3.5 in) to read as one at any size. The sparkline's last point carries the dot because that is how every chart in the product marks its latest close. Nothing in the mark is decorative.

Backgrounds

Card strokes take the surface's ink colour; the front card's fill takes the surface itself, so the fan stays legible. Teal shifts to #3FBFAD on dark. One-colour is for stamps, embossing, and anywhere teal cannot survive.

Size and clear space

Keep clear space of at least half the mark's height on every side. Minimum sizes: 16 px for the mark, 20 px mark height for the horizontal lockup. Below 16 px use the favicon, which drops the back card.

Motion

The mark deals itself: back card in from the left, front card in from the right, then the sparkline draws and the dot lands. Played once it is an entrance; looped — line drawing, holding, clearing from the left while the dot holds to the end — it is the product's loading indicator. Reload this page to replay.

Misuse

The mark is drawn, not styled. If it needs an effect to work on a surface, the surface is wrong.

Don't recolour the cards or the line
Don't rotate the lockup or change the fan
Don't stretch — scale both axes together
Don't place on busy or gradient fields
