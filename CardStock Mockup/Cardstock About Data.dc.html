Sources & coverage

Prices come from realized sales only — completed marketplace listings and major auction results. Asking prices never enter an aggregate; the rare Listed figures you see are labeled as such and cover under 5% of rows.

Populations come from the public census reports the grading companies publish monthly. When a grader restates a past census (it happens), we mark the affected window on charts rather than silently rewriting history.

Excluded: bulk lots, listings with ambiguous grade or damage notes, sales where the card can't be matched to one printing, and marketplaces whose sold data we can't verify. Coverage is deepest for English-language cards graded PSA, BGS, CGC, SGC, ACE, and TAG, plus raw sales.

The April 2025 seam

Before April 2025 our archive holds monthly aggregates — averages and sale counts, back to August 2023. From April 2025 forward we keep the per-sale ledger: every individual transaction with its date, venue, and grade.

That boundary is drawn as a marker on charts. Indicators that need individual sales — churn, price dispersion, Amihud illiquidity, cross-marketplace gap, discount-to-list — can only be computed after it, which is why their history starts there and why backtests refuse to reach further back (the "honest floor").

Refresh & closed months

Sales data refreshes daily; census data lands when graders publish, roughly monthly. The footer stamp on every page tells you how fresh what you're looking at is.

Monthly series only include closed months. The current month appears as a hollow, dashed point — it aggregates the sales recorded so far and will keep revising until the month closes. We never project or extrapolate it.

Sufficiency rules

An indicator that doesn't have enough history to be trustworthy is locked, with its unlock date shown — not rendered anyway with a warning buried in a tooltip. The rules:

Needs
Churn 30d

Weekly bars
~6 months of ledger (≈ Jan 2027)
Daily bars
~12 months of ledger, liquid cards only
Oscillators (RSI, z-score)
their full warm-up window of monthly closes before the first value renders
Seasonality
one observed cycle so far — labeled illustrative until there are three
Composites (G1–G4)
every component signal individually sufficient — a composite never fires on partial inputs

Honesty policy

Some things we simply don't show:

  • No projected or extrapolated data points — a partial month renders as partial, never as a forecast.
  • Backtests start at each screen's honest floor — the first date every filter in it could actually be computed — not at the start of our archive.
  • A backtest that mostly found one set's moment says so, instead of presenting it as a repeatable pattern.
  • Missing metadata renders as METADATA PENDING, not as a silent blank or a guess.
  • When a grader restates history, the restatement window stays visibly marked.
