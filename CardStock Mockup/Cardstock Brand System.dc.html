Logo

Two cards fanned, the front one charting. The mark is stroke-drawn at the app's hairline weight; the sparkline is the only colored element. Wordmark is Inter Bold, tracked tight — the same voice as the nav.

05

Voice

Matter-of-fact and openly nerdy. Precise numbers over adjectives. Jargon is welcome, explained once. Keyboard shortcuts are copy. No exclamation marks, no emoji; dry humor lives in empty states only.
