Privacy Terms of use

Privacy

What we store. Your account email, your binder (positions, cost basis, transactions), your watchlists and saved screens, and display preferences (theme, color-vision mode). That's the list.

What we don't do. We don't sell or share your data, run ads, or use third-party trackers. Analytics are limited to aggregate, anonymous usage counts. Your binder is visible to you alone; no aggregate we publish can be traced back to a person's holdings.

Cookies & local storage. We use local storage for sign-in sessions and preferences — nothing that follows you around the web.

Deletion. Delete your account from Profile & settings and everything above is removed within 30 days. Export your binder as CSV first if you want a copy.
